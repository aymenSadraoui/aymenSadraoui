# 💫 About Me:
Hi, I’m Aymen, a Data Scientist & Machine Learning Engineer at Deemea in Paris, with a PhD in AI from CentraleSupélec. I build deep learning models and production data pipelines for medical imaging, from gigapixel pathology slides to CT, MRI and X-ray. I care about models that are calibrated, robust across hospitals and reproducible.<br><br>About Me<br><br>🔭 I’m currently working on: automated data pipelines connected to hospital PACS (DICOM), plus deep learning for image quality control, segmentation and diagnosis support in multi-centric clinical studies.<br><br>👯 I’m looking to collaborate on: trustworthy medical AI, such as few-shot learning, algorithm unrolling and computational pathology, and open research that connects clinicians and ML engineers.<br><br>🤝 I’m looking for help with: scaling MLOps for multi-site data, namely drift detection, calibration monitoring and reproducible benchmarking of out-of-distribution generalization.<br><br>🌱 I’m currently learning: more MLOps and cloud deployment, to move research prototypes into monitored, containerized production services.<br><br>💬 Ask me about: deep learning for histopathology and radiology, turning optimization algorithms into neural networks, medical data engineering, and the path from a PhD to industry.<br><br>⚡ Fun fact: my team took 1st place out of 256 teams in the Lung Transplant Rejection data challenge, and outside of work I swim, garden and love cinema.
