# 💫 About Me

I am a Data Scientist and Machine Learning Engineer holding a PhD in Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence from CentraleSupélec, Université Paris-Saclay. My background is in medical image analysis, where I have designed and deployed deep learning and machine learning models and scalable data pipelines for high-dimensional, multimodal data, from gigapixel histopathology slides to radiology scans (CT, MRI, X-ray). I currently work at Deemea (Paris, France).<br>
While my experience was built in healthcare, the methods behind it are domain-agnostic: computer vision, representation learning, data engineering, MLOps, and rigorous model evaluation. I am open to applying them to new challenges across industries and applications, and to roles in data science, machine learning, AI engineering, and MLOps.<br><br>
🔭 **Currently working on:** end-to-end data ingestion and processing pipelines, and deep learning models for quality control, segmentation, and classification, deployed as containerized services in production.<br><br>
🤝 **Open to collaboration on:** applied AI and computer vision projects in any sector, including few-shot learning and learning from limited labeled data, and research that turns advanced methods into reliable products.<br><br>
🔎 **Seeking opportunities in:** data science, machine learning engineering, AI engineering, and MLOps, in any field where robust, scalable, and well-evaluated AI creates value.<br><br>
🌱 **Currently developing expertise in:** MLOps and cloud deployment, to move models from prototype to monitored, scalable production systems.<br><br>
🛠️ **Core skills:** Python, PyTorch, OpenCV, Scikit-learn, Docker, MLflow, CI/CD, Linux, ETL/ELT pipelines, model calibration, data-drift detection, and out-of-distribution generalization.<br><br>
💬 **Ask me about:** deep learning and computer vision, building production-grade data pipelines, MLOps best practices, turning optimization algorithms into neural networks, and moving from academic research to industry.<br><br>
⚡ **Distinction:** 1st place out of 256 teams in the Lung Transplant Rejection data challenge, organized by Hôpital Foch and the Health Data Hub (2024).
